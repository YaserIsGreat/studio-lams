# Studio Lams

Studio Lams is a project of mine that builds websites and digital systems for local businesses that have little to no online presence. The goal is simple: take businesses that are running entirely offline and give them the digital infrastructure they need to grow, compete and run more efficiently.

## The Mission

Most local businesses are good at what they do but have nothing online. No website, no booking system, no way for customers to reach them outside of walking in. Studio Lams fixes that, starting from the ground up.

## Current Stage

The first client is ready to go live. A fully built and deployed website for a local convenience store, including store information, opening hours, contact form with email forwarding, scroll animations and a mobile responsive layout.

## Where This Is Going

The plan is to scale this across more local businesses and build out a much wider range of digital services, including:

Custom websites tailored to each business
Online booking and appointment systems
AI powered chat assistants for customer enquiries
Loyalty and rewards programme integrations
Inventory and stock management tools
Social media scheduling and automation
Delivery and order management systems
Ongoing support and maintenance packages

The focus is on businesses that do not have these systems at all, giving them real infrastructure rather than just a pretty page.
